EVERY FRAME COMPUTED BY THE ENGINE

Watch one merge, slowly.

Each panel is drawn as an occupation ladder: a rung per shell, its pin on the left, its symbols as filled slots. When two ladders dock, the shared symbols pair against the pins and that web of pairings collapses into a determinant — the overlap scalar. Panels, scalars and signs below are all baked from SandhiCanon by bake_chamber.py.
