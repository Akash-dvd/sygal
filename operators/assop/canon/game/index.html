HOW TO PLAY

Split. Match. Fuse.

  1. Sandhi: select two panels with the same upper blade and at least one shared lower symbol.
  2. Viccheda: select a chain of three or more symbols to split it into two overlapping panels.
  3. Parivṛtti: reverse one plate to face its seam the other way. A grade-k plate reverses with the sign (−1)k(k−1)/2, so grade 2 and 3 flip while grade 4 does not.
  4. Close a cycle: adding a panel whose two ends already lie in a chain completes an alternating cycle and clears it.

This is a visual teaching game inspired by SandhiCanon. The Python engine additionally tracks grades, parity signs, recursive contractions, and Capelli overlap coefficients.
