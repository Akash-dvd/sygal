CANDIDATE VISUAL GRAMMAR

A small alphabet, recursively composed.

01 · SYNTAX 
Vector ::= grade-1 atom
Blade  ::= Vector | Wedge(Vector, Vector...)
Expr   ::= Blade | LContract(Blade, Expr)
Germ   ::= Sign · Scalar · Expr

Packet ::= Direction · VectorBeads
Body   ::= Packet | Shell(Vector, Body)
Flat   ::= GroupedGate(Blade, Body)
02 · ENCODING

Vectors become ordered beads

A∧B∧C becomes one directed genome packet A—B—C. Each symbol is grade 1; the arrow fixes wedge order.

03 · RECURSION

Contractions become shell gates

d₁⌋(A∧d₂⌋B) becomes shell d₁ containing packet A and shell d₂ containing B.

04 · ACCOUNTING

Scalars never hide in decoration

Every coefficient, sign, overlap and Capelli factor is a visible token attached to the germ or mutation seam.

NON-NEGOTIABLE INVARIANTS

Unique reading

One rendered body must decode to one normalized expression plus its explicit coefficient.

No free reversal

Changing genome direction changes the sign whenever the blade-reversal parity is odd.

No free cut

Viccheda produces children and a scalar-debt token; it cannot silently duplicate a seam.

No visual-only merge

Sticky ends may suggest a match, but only SandhiCanon can authorize the resulting residual.

No general versors

Atomic symbols cannot be rotors, spinors or mixed-grade multivectors. Higher blades are built only by wedging vectors.
