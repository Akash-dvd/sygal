`Nr < Dr` is the residual of `Dr` after contraction by `Nr`: `Nr` extracts the matching part, and the surviving part of `Dr` remains.

In this sense it behaves idempotently with respect to the same match: once `Nr` has extracted what it can from `Dr`, repeating the same extraction gives nothing new.

In the germ universe, `Nr` is the active virus / extractor and `Dr` is the larger host body being acted on.

So `Nr < Dr` reads as: the virus consumes or removes the matching patch of the host, and the residual host body remains.

By contrast, sandhi is better read as a symbiotic fusion: two compatible germs dock on a shared seam, the shared overlap collapses out, and one residual strain continues.
